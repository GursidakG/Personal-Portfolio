# Surface brief: index.html (hero and assistant)

Scope: the single-page portfolio; this round extended the hero and added the holographic assistant. Mode: Experience for the hero, Read for the rest.

Audience: recruiters, hiring managers, Redstone Automations clients, classmates, crawlers. Job: understand who Gursidak is in one viewport, verify, reach out. Proof: résumé facts, three shipped projects, measurable internship results. Constraint: static site, no backend, no API keys; every claim traces to the résumé or the owner.

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** the hero is a person you can talk to. A vector Gursidak stands in the frame, asks to be clicked, taps his watch and projects a holographic assistant that answers from the page. It refuses the static portrait-plus-headline hero.
- **OWN-WORLD:** the incumbent warm world (cream #f4e9da, paper #f9f3ea, espresso #2e1f16, caramel #c4813f, rust #a85c32; Fraunces display, DM Sans text) plus one new material: amber hologram light. Espresso glass panel, caramel edge light, soft scanlines, cream type. The character is flat geometric vector: navy dastar, thin glasses, dark beard, sage-green sweater, dark trousers, a watch on the left wrist.
- **STORY:** the visitor reads the name and roles, sees the figure say "Click on me", clicks, watches him tap his watch, a panel rises, they ask a question and get an answer with links deeper into the page, then scroll on to the proof.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** left column: status line, name in display serif, three roles, one-line description, Contact and Resume buttons, location. Right column: full-body character on a thin ground line at the bottom left of a 580px stage, speech bubble by the head, hologram anchored at the wrist rising to the top right. Affiliation strip below.
- **FORM:** pinned by the owner's request (animated self, watch, hologram); no concept roll was run. Seed key: none; extension of an established surface.
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Memorable moment: the watch tap and the panel resolving from blur, lit from the wrist.
Unresolved: domain (canonical, OG image), résumé PDF still says AIVAA, availability line, per-project links.
